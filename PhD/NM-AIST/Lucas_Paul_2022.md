# Lucas Paul (2022)

**Title:** Structural and Biophysical Characterization of Cassava Linamarase and the Role of Solvents on Linamarin’s Properties: A Computational Study

**Institution:** Nelson Mandela African Institution of Science and Technology (NM-AIST), Arusha, Tanzania  
**Degree:** Doctor of Philosophy in Materials Science and Engineering  
**Date:** June 2022  
**Source:** [Original NM-AIST full thesis](https://dspace.nm-aist.ac.tz/server/api/core/bitstreams/d027533d-89b8-4904-a6d6-16d7cc768210/content)

## Research overview

The thesis investigates the structure of cassava linamarase, its interactions with linamarin, and solvent-dependent conformational behaviour using homology modelling, molecular docking, molecular dynamics and binding free-energy analysis.

## Useful writing and methodology lessons

- Builds from a practical cassava-processing and cyanide-related problem toward molecular-level investigation.
- Connects the literature review to distinct computational methods and their validation.
- Provides a comparative example for developing background, problem statement, significance and computational methodology, while keeping interpretation proportionate to measured endpoints.
