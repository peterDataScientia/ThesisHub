# Jacqueline N. Mgaya (2022)

**Title:** Effects of Sample Preservation Methods and Storage Duration on the Performance of Mid-Infrared Spectroscopy for Predicting the Age of Malaria Vectors

**Institution:** Nelson Mandela African Institution of Science and Technology (NM-AIST), Arusha, Tanzania  
**Degree:** Master of Science in Public Health Research  
**Date:** August 2022  
**Source:** [Original NM-AIST repository record](https://dspace.nm-aist.ac.tz/items/ddbe33bb-4d15-4d90-81b5-4b34cdbe2c22)

## Research overview

This dissertation examines how preservation method and storage duration affect the performance of mid-infrared spectroscopy and machine-learning classifiers for predicting the age of malaria vectors. It used laboratory-reared *Anopheles arabiensis* and compared silica desiccant, freezing and ethanol preservation across different storage durations.

## Useful writing and methodology lessons

- Links a public-health surveillance need with a precisely defined prediction-reliability question.
- Separates training/test handling conditions and reports how performance changes when those conditions differ.
- Structures the dissertation from problem and literature review through experimental methods, model evaluation, results and conclusions.
